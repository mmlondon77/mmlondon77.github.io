My research spans structural heart disease, cardiovascular imaging, and methodological innovation, integrating clinical investigation with basic science and translational medicine to bridge mechanistic insight and patient-centered outcomes.

  • Transcatheter valve therapies (TAVI) – prosthesis performance, biomechanics, and post-implant assessment.
  • Subclinical leaflet thrombosis (SLT/HALT) – imaging phenotypes, mechanistic interpretation, and risk modelling.
  • Advanced cardiovascular imaging – MDCT-based geometric analysis and post-procedural evaluation.
  • Basic science & translational medicine – mechanistic pathways, biomarkers, and clinically actionable phenotypes linking laboratory discovery to bedside decision-making.
  • Evidence synthesis & meta-analysis – time-to-event meta-analysis and individual patient data approaches.
  • Interpretable artificial intelligence in medicine – predictive modelling aligned with transparent, clinically meaningful decision support.
